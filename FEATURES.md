## Safety and smart tools
Scam check for new contacts, chat summary, before-you-share check, tone check, evidence log, business leads, expense split, shopping list, saved messages, notes and lists, snippets, important dates, storage cleaner, emergency lock, link scanner. All **Built**. They run on the device.
