## What WhatsApp has that Talkza does not (yet)
- Billions of users, so the people you know are already there.
- Phone-number contacts and discovery.
- Years of testing, and reliable call ring and notifications when the app is closed.
- Backups to Google Drive or iCloud.
- An independently audited encryption protocol (Signal protocol).

## Honest notes
- Both apps encrypt messages end to end. Talkza's design uses the TweetNaCl library and has had no independent security audit.
- Features marked "Verified" were tested by hand by the owner; they are not independently audited.
